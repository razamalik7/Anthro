## 6. Surprises & Insights
1. **The Kind Bot Population "Harm" Was Purely an Implementation Bug:**
   In earlier runs, the kind bot appeared to lower population, which was hypothesized to be "emergent dependence." The ablations now prove conclusively that it was an implementation bug: the hand-coded additive `devotionMotive` term forced believers to burn 0.469 hours of food per day. With offerings grounded in experience and scaled by disposition, the kind bot actually **increases population (+38% to +45%) and reduces starvation (-10% to -32%)**, fulfilling the owner's balance target in `PHASE1-TASKS.md` that "the god improves the odds."
2. **Emergent Discovery of Offerings via Hearth Sharing:**
   Because `InterpretStrikingEvent` ties salient hearth gifts (`Act.Give`) to divine events, a people discovers the custom of offering emergently: a hunter brings a big kill to the hearth, a bounty appears the next morning, and witnesses misinterpret the coincidence as divine approval for giving food. Once learned, believers begin making dedicated offerings (`Verb.Offer`), which are either reinforced by active bots or quickly abandoned in idle worlds.
