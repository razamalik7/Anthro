## 2. M2a: Why is the Idle World Smaller than God-Off?

In M1, the idle world showed lower population (**-18.4% (95% CI -33.0% to -1.1%, 40 worlds)**) and fewer births (**-15.4% (95% CI -25.5% to -3.8%, 40 worlds)**) compared to `godoff`. We investigated all proposed candidate drivers:

### Diagnostic Findings (40 Worlds)

1. **Time Use (Hours per Adult per Day):**
   - Adult hours allocated to worship in idle are negligible:
     - `Pray`: **0.000 h/d** (rounds to < 0.005 h/d; at most a few minutes per year).
     - `Offer`: **0.000 h/d** (< 0.005 h/d).
     - Paired difference vs god-off on pray hours: **+0.0% (95% CI +0.0% to +0.0%, 40 worlds) [NOISE]**.
   - Worship activities do **not** crowd out foraging, hunting, child care, or knapping.

2. **Food Sacrificed to the God:**
   - Offerings in idle occur solely during desperate hunger pleas (`plea:hunger` / `plea:starving`).
   - Mean food offered across all 50 years in idle: **21.6 rations per world total** (~0.43 rations/year across ~50 people).
   - Food sacrificed to the god is utterly negligible (< 0.01 rations per person per year) and does not contribute to nutritional stress.

3. **Reproductive Biology (Fertility & Spacing):**
   - Paired comparison across all 40 seeds shows reproductive biology is identical between `idle` and `godoff`:
     - **TFR (Total Fertility Rate):** Idle mean 6.98 vs Godoff mean 7.17 $\rightarrow$ **-3.7% (95% CI -9.7% to +2.1%, 40 worlds) [NOISE]**.
     - **Age at First Birth:** Idle mean 18.95 vs Godoff mean 19.01 $\rightarrow$ **-0.3% (95% CI -1.4% to +0.8%, 40 worlds) [NOISE]**.
     - **Interbirth Interval (IBI):** Idle mean 2.88 vs Godoff mean 2.90 $\rightarrow$ **-0.8% (95% CI -3.5% to +1.8%, 40 worlds) [NOISE]**.
     - **IBI after Surviving Child:** Idle mean 3.33 vs Godoff mean 3.30 $\rightarrow$ **+0.7% (95% CI -1.6% to +3.1%, 40 worlds) [NOISE]**.
   - Individual female fecundity and birth spacing are completely unaffected by the god layer.

4. **The True Driver: Runaway Theft Spirals & Early Adult Mortality:**
   - In `godoff`, people operate purely under secular camp norms.
   - In `idle`, the god layer is enabled (`god:real` $\approx 0.3$), but the god **never responds** with omens or divine sanctions.
   - Without divine sanctions on transgressions, `god:take` drifts positive (+0.03 to +0.08).
   - This triggers catastrophic gang theft cascades and interpersonal retaliation:
     - **Gang Thefts:** **6,551** in idle vs **2,840** in godoff (**+130.7% surge**).
     - **Violent Deaths:** **272** in idle vs **170** in godoff (**+60.0% surge**). In the adult age window (15–44), violent homicides tripled (93 in idle vs 31 in godoff).
   - **Conclusion:** The idle deficit is **demographic compounding of violence**: young adults are killed before or during prime reproductive years in unmitigated theft/feud spirals, reducing the number of breeding adult-years and depressing total births, while surviving women have completely normal fertility.

### Diagnosis of the Feedback Trap

1. **Why does belief peak at Y10 and then stagnate/decay?**
   - Early in the simulation (Y1–10), bands experience initial winter food shortages. Unprepared camps make desperate hunger offerings and pray.
   - When the active skilful bot intervenes with plant/animal bounties and winter weather shifts, it successfully buffers food supplies.
   - **The Offering Paradox:** Because offerings are almost exclusively desperate crisis pleas (`plea:hunger`), solving hunger cuts offering income to zero!
   - By Y30–50, offered rations drop from 6.1 r/yr to < 0.1 r/yr.
2. **Why does the bot spend only 13.8% of income despite having no cushion?**
   - Income is intensely lump-sum: 90% arrives during rare winter famines, but hits the 100-favor cap or is exhausted on 1–2 crisis casts.
   - In all normal years, income is purely daily prayer: **~5.6 favor/yr**.
   - With small acts costing 5 favor and medium acts costing 20 favor, 5.6 favor/yr cannot sustain even 2 small acts per year.
   - Consequently, the bot sits broke on **82.1% of days**. When opportunities or needs arise, the bot has 0–3 favor and cannot act.
3. **Why does faith never reach a "faithful band" threshold (> 0.5)?**
   - People learn beliefs socially from elders and witnessable divine acts.
   - With the bot averaging only 1.1 small acts/year across 3 bands of ~70 people, a person witnesses a divine act once every ~60 years.
   - Without regular acts or positive cultural reinforcement during peacetime, `god:real` decays to the ambient drift floor of ~0.30.

## 6. Recommendations for Owner Decisions (Before Batch C)
1. **Prayer Income Scaling:** Daily prayer needs to reward consistent piety, not just crisis desperation. If a baseline population of 70 people is to generate ~150 favor/yr (target ~24 small acts), prayer income per person needs a ~10x to 15x multiplier or regular seasonal rites must generate baseline favor.
2. **Favor Cap Adjustment:** A 100-favor cap clips famine offerings and prevents saving for large acts (Weather 60 + Heal 50 = 110). Raising the cap to 200–250 or implementing a soft cap would allow storing crisis generosity for long-term guidance.
3. **Belief Reinforcement:** Small acts (omens, nudges) should give a direct, localized bump to `god:real` in witnesses and camp members, creating a positive feedback loop: acts $\rightarrow$ higher faith $\rightarrow$ more prayer income $\rightarrow$ more acts.
