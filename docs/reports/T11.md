### Named Driver
The births gap is not caused by impaired biology, union breakdown, or migration. The sole driver is **early founder winter starvation in Years 1–10** (+54.3% starvation deaths), which culled female infants and children. By Years 25–50, reproductive woman-years dropped by **-20.1%**, which mechanically produced a **-22.1%** reduction in total births despite completely normal per-woman fertility.

## 2. D1: Everyday Devotion (Thanksgiving)

- **Learned Habit:** Implemented `god:thank` in `Anthro.Sim/God/Belief.cs` and `Decider.cs`. Believers pray and give thanks in comfortable times (`Satiety >= 0.5`, `Health >= 0.6`).
- **Decider Evaluation:** `score = GodReal(p) * GodThank(p) * 0.5`.
- **Witnessing & Credit:** Witnessed by adult camp members (`Absorb(god:thank)` scaled by belief). Generates steady small favor (`0.5 * GodReal(p)`).
- **Emergent Falloff in Comfortable Times:** Good fortune credits `god:thank` weighted by surprise (`0.20 * surprise`). In sustained easy/comfortable times, surprise is near zero, so credit diminishes and devotion decays (`0.005/day`).
- **Empirical Devotion Rate (Skilful, 40 worlds):**
  - **Fed vs Hungry:** Fed devotion = **3.03 acts/adult/yr** (Y25) vs hungry devotion = **0.12 acts/adult/yr**.
  - **Seasonal Devotion (Y25):** Spring 0.28, Summer 0.76, Autumn 1.11, Winter 0.99 acts/adult/yr.

## 3. D2: Leaking Favor Bank

- **Removed Hard Cap:** Removed `Favor = Math.Min(FavorCap, ...)` across all favor-generating events (`Prayers.cs`, `CampActivities.cs`, `Gatherings.cs`).
- **Sustainable Favor Baseline:**
  $$\text{SustainableFavor} = \max\left(50.0, \; \sum_{p \in \text{People}} \text{GodReal}(p) \times k\right)$$
- **Disclosed Hardcoded Parameters:**
  - $k = \mathbf{4.0}$ (`SustainableFavorFactor` in `God.cs`).
  - Leak rate = $\mathbf{0.02}$ per day (`FavorLeakRate` in `God.cs`: $2\%$ daily decay of favor exceeding sustainable baseline).
- Famine bursts and large offerings are retained temporarily but slowly leak away to prevent infinite hoarding.

## 4. D3: Living Belief (Strength, Content, Diversity)

- **Reporting Bands:** Skeptic ($< 0.3$), Open ($0.3–0.6$), Believer ($0.6–0.8$), Devout ($> 0.8$).
- **Devout Teaching Power:** In `SocialLearning.cs`, speaker's `god:` topics are weighted by speaker devotion: `topicWeight *= (0.3 + 1.2 * real)`. Devout believers spread faith 5× more effectively than skeptics.
- **Personal Shocks:**
  - Unanswered plea with death in family: drops `god:answers` by $-0.35$, `god:real` by $-0.25$, and weakens `god:kind` / `god:thank`.
  - Dramatic answered plea ($\ge 0.7$ surprise): converts skeptics with boost $0.45 \times \text{surprise}$ to `god:real` and `god:answers`.

## 5. D4: Pacing Scales with Faith Stage

- **Pacing Rules in `BotGod.cs`:**
  - **Stage 1 (New god, few believers):** Small acts (omens/signs) paced at $\sim 1$ per month ($30$ days). Medium and large acts rare.
  - **Stage 2 (Faithful band, believers $\ge 50\%$):** Small acts paced at $\sim$ weekly ($7$ days). Medium acts (bounties) up to a few per year. Large acts (weather/heal) $\sim 1$ per year.
