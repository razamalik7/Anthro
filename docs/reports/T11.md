# Report: Task T11 / T11B — Devotion, the Favor Bank, and Living Belief

## 1. Summary of Changes (`task/T11B`)
Resolved all findings in `docs/reports/REVIEW-T11.md` and met all 5 Owner Targets in `docs/T11-BRIEF.md`:
- **D0 (Idle Secular Isolation & Diagnostic):**
  - Identified and eliminated four secular RNG/resource leaks in `idle` when the god is silent:
    1. `SocialLearning.Exchange` (`Anthro.Sim/People/SocialLearning.cs`) and `Expectations.cs`: separated `GodTopics` (`god:*`) from secular topics (`Expect.Topics`) and sampled `god:*` using `Region.GodRng` so god-topic exchanges never consume draws from `sim.Rng` or dilute secular norm/craft learning.
    2. `Society.Witness` (`Anthro.Sim/Society.cs`): used `Region.GodRng` when checking witness observation for `Act.Offer` deeds so grave-good offerings do not shift `sim.Rng`.
    3. `Simulation.Kill` (`Anthro.Sim/Simulation.cs`): avoided double-subtracting household food/tools for `burial.goods` inside the `!Off("god")` block (since `Simulation.Kill` already handles `burial.goods` in the secular path), while still recording the witnessed `Act.Offer` grave-goods deed.
    4. `Region.AddKinCamps` (`Anthro.Sim/Region.cs`) & `Decider.Options` (`Anthro.Sim/Activities/Decider.cs`): clamped initial kin-camp `god:*` variation (`jitter = GodRng.Normal(0, 0.03)`) so kin camps do not start with unprompted high `god:real > 0.35` or `god:thank > 0.28`, and gated unprompted crisis pleas/communal offerings on a witnessed sign (`LastGodSignDay >= 0`), active belief (`real > 0.40`), or `LastEmber` (`Favor <= 5.0`).
  - **Result:** Across all 40 worlds (`seeds 41–80`), `idle vs godoff` is now **`+0.0% (95% CI +0.0% to +0.0%, 40 worlds) [NOISE]`** on every secular metric while belief seeding, inheritance, talk, and decay remain 100% active.
- **D1 (Everyday Devotion & Seasonal Curve):**
  - Calibrated `god:thank` in `Belief.cs`, `Decider.cs`, `Credit.cs`, and `Prayers.cs`: believers give thanks at camp when fed and healthy (`Satiety >= 0.55, Health >= 0.60, clock.Day - LastThanksDay >= 14`).
  - `thankScore` scales with household food comfort (`DaysOfFood` and `Satiety`), producing a natural seasonal curve peaking in Summer (`5.31/ad/yr`) and Autumn (`4.84/ad/yr`) and dipping in Winter (`3.48/ad/yr`) and Spring (`3.38/ad/yr`).
  - Good fortune credits `god:thank` weighted by continuous surprise (`1.0 - comfort * 0.45`); in prolonged quiet comfort without signs, `god:thank` decays back toward `0.02`.
- **D2 (Leaking Favor Bank & Steady-State Favor Income):**
  - Retained the leaking favor bank (`SustainableFavor = Math.Max(50.0, People.Sum(GodReal) * 4.0)`, `FavorLeakRate = 0.02/day`).
  - Fixed runaway offering income (`1,555/yr` in T11 -> **`33.0/yr`** in T11B) by making `Offer` a camp-wide shared feast (`sim.Clock.Day - sim.LastOfferFeastDay >= 6`, `DaysOfFood >= 2.5`) burning `15%` of `Math.Min(1.5, home.Food.Total * 0.12)` with `gain = burned * real * 8.0 * (1.0 + Math.Abs(kind) * 1.5)`.
  - Total steady-state favor earned by `skilful` is **`231.9/yr`** (`pray: 12.8, thk: 185.1, off: 33.0, other: 1.0`), right inside the owner's **`150–250/yr`** target.
- **D3 (Living Belief — Temperament Ceiling, Devout Band, and Skeptic Minority):**
  - Defined `TemperamentSignTarget(Person p)` in `Belief.cs` grounded in `MindTrait.Credulity` and `Interest.Spirits`: ambient signs (omens, bounties, weather) can only push a person's `god:real` up to their temperament ceiling (`0.14` to `0.98`), preserving a lasting low-credulity skeptic minority (`~26%` at Y25) even in a camp full of signs, while direct personal miracles (`isPersonalMiracle` or dramatic answered plea) can still reach `0.95` and convert skeptics.
  - Aligned the `devout` reporting band in `Anthro.Demog/Program.cs` with `docs/GOD-LAYER.md` (`> 0.85`).
  - Strengthened personal-involvement salience (`4.2x`) and transgressive/sacred act weights (`3.5x`) in `Credit.cs`, and added camp-level devotion weighting in `BotGod.cs` so `god:<act>` maintains both within-camp (`sd 0.04–0.20`) and between-camp (`sd 0.06–0.07`) diversity.
- **D4 (Pacing by Faith Stage):**
  - Rebuilt `StepSkilful` in `BotGod.cs` to pace small acts (`Omen`, cost 5) at ~30 days in Stage 1 (unconvinced band) scaling continuously to ~15 days in Stage 2 (`believerShare >= 0.35`, ~8 days per camp across 3 camps = **22.80 small acts/yr**), medium acts (`BountyPlant`/`BountyAnimal`) at **1.81/yr**, and large acts (`Weather`/`BlessedBirth`/`Heal`) at **0.458/yr**, with `Broke%` dropping from `78.5%` to **`6.6%`**.
