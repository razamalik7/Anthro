2. **C2 (The 5 Pattern Bots in `BotGod.cs`):**
   - **Charity (`StepCharity`):** Monitors witnessed sharing (`Act.Give`). When a person shares, rewards the camp with `BountyPlant` / `BountyAnimal` or an `Omen`. Teaches people that the god loves generosity.
   - **Demanding (`StepDemanding`):** Monitors communal offerings (`Act.Offer`). When offerings are made, responds favorably with bounties; when offerings lapse for extended periods, visits the camp with harsh weather. Teaches people that the god demands gifts.
   - **Malevolent (`StepMalevolent`):** Sends destructive weather and lightning bolts. Eases destruction only when terrified camps make offerings to appease divine wrath. Teaches people that offerings hold back the god's anger.
   - **Stern (`StepStern`):** Tracks camp wrongdoing (`Act.Attack` and `Act.Steal`). Strikes wrongdoers with lightning (when $\text{GodHarm} \ge 4.0$) or terrifying omens. Teaches people that the god punishes wickedness.
   - **Capricious (`StepCapricious`):** Dispatches random powers (omens, mild weather, small bounties) unpredictably without moral or behavioral correlation. Produces shifting superstitions and doubt.

## 6. Evaluation: Did Each Pattern Teach Its Expected Religion?

1. **Charity ("the god loves generosity"):**
   - **Pattern Taught:** **YES.** By rewarding witnessed gifts with bounties, people learned that `god:give` (`+0.67`) and `god:kind` (`+0.65`) carry massive divine favor. Sacrifice offerings were not culturally expected (`god:offer +0.07`).
   - **Emergent Outcome:** Generosity fostered food sharing and reduced starvation by `-10.2% (95% CI -18.7% to -0.7%)` [REAL].
2. **Demanding ("the god demands gifts"):**
   - **Pattern Taught:** **YES.** Communal offerings became a cultural cornerstone (`god:offer` reached `+0.51`), earning `123.6` favor/yr in sacrificial offerings.
   - **Emergent Outcome:** Communal feast-offerings suppressed gang thefts by `-56.5% (95% CI -75.2% to -24.3%)` [REAL] and stimulated camp mobility (`+44.7%` camp moves [REAL]).
3. **Malevolent ("offerings hold back its anger"):**
   - **Pattern Taught:** **YES.** `GodHarm` reached `430.1` and `god:kind` dropped to `-0.48`. Violent deaths surged by `+194.7% (95% CI +128.1% to +277.9%)` [REAL] from punitive lightning smites and storms. People learned that sacrifices appease wrath (`god:offer +0.47`), while thanks withered (`god:thank +0.05`).
4. **Stern ("the god punishes wrongdoing"):**
   - **Pattern Taught:** **YES.** Lightning strikes smiting brawlers and thieves resulted in `+142.1%` violent deaths [REAL], but completely suppressed gang thefts by **`-71.8% (95% CI -83.1% to -54.0%)` [REAL]**. People internalized anti-wrongdoing norms (`god:take -0.27`, `god:attack -0.08`).
5. **Capricious ("shifting superstitions, doubt"):**
   - **Pattern Taught:** **YES.** Perceived responsiveness collapsed to `god:answers +0.04` (lowest of all gods). Bands moved frequently in search of signs (`+22.5%` camp moves [REAL]), while all other secular metrics were indistinguishable from baseline noise.
