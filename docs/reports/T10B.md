## 3. Offering Pathways & Cultural Fading Analysis

### 1. In how many worlds offering appears
- **Set 1 (41–60):**
  - `idle`: **18 / 20 worlds** had offerings (total food offered: 733.7 rations).
  - `skilful`: **18 / 20 worlds** had offerings (total food offered: 3,858.9 rations).
- **Set 2 (61–80):**
  - `idle2`: **20 / 20 worlds** had offerings (total food offered: 87.7 rations).
  - `skilful2`: **20 / 20 worlds** had offerings (total food offered: 6,118.7 rations).

### 2. By which path offering emerges
1. **Cultural Seed (B4):** ~25% of founding bands start with a weak offering habit (`baseOffer = 0.05 - 0.15`) rolled from `Region.GodRng`. This provides initial variation across cultures.
2. **Escalated Plea (B2) — The Main Driver:** When desperate crises occur (hunger, illness, injury) and prayers go unanswered for $\ge 1$ day, petitioners bargain with the unseen by offering food even without prior learned expectation. This is the primary engine generating new offerings in faithful camps.
3. **Grave Goods (B3):** When `burial.rites` is known, adult kin place food/tools with their dead as a gift to the unseen. In unseeded 50-year worlds without tech grants, `burial.rites` is rarely discovered organically (0 discoveries across these runs), but functions reliably in tech starts and unit tests.

### 3. Whether offering fades when nothing answers
- **In Idle Worlds (No God Acts):**
  - **Set 1 (41–60):** Mean `god:offer` starts at **0.021** in Year 1 and erodes down to **0.003** by Year 50 (**86% decay**).
  - **Set 2 (61–80):** Mean `god:offer` starts at **0.017** in Year 1 and erodes down to **0.002** by Year 50 (**88% decay**).
  - When offerings go unanswered by divine signs, daily erosion (`0.02/day` for unanswered offering deeds, `0.005/day` baseline) erodes the belief back toward zero.
- **In Skilful Worlds (God Acts & Rewards):**
  - In `skilful`, seasonal bounties and timely relief answer emergency pleas, validating offerings.
  - Belief is actively sustained, and offering feasts continue throughout all 50 years, burning **3,858.9 rations** in Set 1 and **6,118.7 rations** in Set 2 (generating steady favor for the god).

## 5. Discussion of Large Results

### 1. Near-Elimination of Gang Thefts Under Skilful
- **Set 1 (41–60):** Gang thefts dropped from **75.2/yr** in godoff and **79.7/yr** in idle down to **6.8/yr** under skilful (**−91.0% reduction**).
- **Set 2 (61–80):** Gang thefts dropped from **247.9/yr** in idle2 down to **66.0/yr** under skilful2 (**−73.4% reduction**).
- **Why this happens:** Under B1, sharing teaches `god:give`, reinforcing communal generosity rather than mistaken sacrificial rites. Meanwhile, the skilful bot uses paced omens against theft and violence, driving cultural norms strongly negative against taking (`G:Take = -0.09` on Set 1, `-0.15` on Set 2). Gang raids virtually disappear.

### 2. Demographic Expansion and Extinction Safety on Set 2
- On Set 2, `skilful2` achieved the strongest performance recorded across all phases:
  - Median population: **65.0** (vs 56.5 in baseline, **+15.0%**).
  - Total population: **1,539** (vs 1,267 in baseline, **+21.5%**).
  - Near-extinction dips below 15: **0 / 20 worlds** (vs 2 in baseline).
  - Extinctions: **0 / 20 worlds**.
  - Violent deaths: cut in half (**3.0/yr** vs 6.2/yr in idle2).
- On Set 1, `skilful` reached **1,263 total pop** (recovering +28.1% over idle's 986 pop), with violent deaths halved to **3.4/yr**.

### 3. Fading of Belief When Offerings Go Unanswered
- In idle worlds where no god responds, the custom of offering dies out naturally:
  - Set 1: `god:offer` fell from **0.021** to **0.003**.
  - Set 2: `god:offer` fell from **0.017** to **0.002**.
- People abandon futile rituals without any arbitrary timers or hardcoded cooldowns, strictly through continuous outcome learning and unrewarded decay.

## 6. Audit & Disclosed Constants
- **`Anthro.Sim/God/Credit.cs`:**
  - Removed `Act.Give` from teaching `god:offer`. `Act.Give` strictly teaches `god:give`.
- **`Anthro.Sim/Activities/Decider.cs`:**
  - Escalated plea criteria: `openPrayer != null`, `Clock.Day > openPrayer.StartDay`, `desperation > 0.35`, `home.Food.Total >= 0.5`.
  - Escalated plea score formula: `desperation * real * (0.6 + credulity * 0.4)`.
  - Escalated plea gift amount: `Math.Min(1.0, Math.Max(0.2, home.Food.Total * 0.15))`.
- **`Anthro.Sim/God/Prayers.cs`:**
  - Answered escalated plea outcome learning weight: `0.20 * (0.4 + cred * 0.8) * Math.Max(0.2, real)` for petitioner; `0.10` for camp witnesses.
  - Failed/abandoned escalated plea decrement: `-1.0` target with weight `0.15 * (0.4 + cred * 0.8)`.
- **`Anthro.Sim/Simulation.cs` (Kill):**
  - Grave goods left when `burial.rites` or `burial.goods` is known: up to 1.0 food taken from hearth (or 1 tool), creating an `Act.Offer` deed.

  - Cultural seed: 25% chance of weak offering habit `baseOffer = rng.Range(0.05, 0.15)` per people, drawn from `Region.GodRng`.
