### Core Question: Is the skilful bot short of favor, or does it spend badly?

**Answer: It is a structural mismatch between the favor cap and burst power costs.**

1. **The economy is NOT too tight to produce favor:**
   Petitioner prayers and food offerings generate **~915–1,015 favor per year** under the skilful bot (over 45,000–50,000 total favor per 50-year run!). People are constantly praying (7.3–7.5 favor/yr) and offering food (905–1,005 favor/yr).
2. **The bot spends only ~5–7% of the gross favor generated (~50–65 / yr out of ~1,000 / yr):**
   The bot is not overspending in aggregate; it spends only a tiny fraction of what the tribe offers.
3. **Yet the god is broke on ~68–71% of all days (every world goes broke by Year 1–2, Day 335–496):**
   Why? Because `FavorCap = Math.Max(50.0, believers.Sum(GodReal) * 10)`. With 5–15 adult believers, the pool is capped at **50–150 favor**:
   - Heavy harvest offerings (50–200 favor) immediately hit the low cap and discard 90%+ of the surplus.
   - When the bot acts, it uses expensive burst powers: `Weather` costs **60 favor**, `BountyAnimal` costs **30 favor**, `Heal` costs **50 favor**.
   - A single late-autumn weather intervention (60 favor) instantly zeroes out the pool.
   - Once at zero, the god remains below 5.0 favor for weeks to months until the next prayer/offering arrives, preventing even cheap 5-favor omens.
4. **Summary:**
   The skilful bot's problem is **structural burst-starvation**. The cap clips the abundant favor that people generate, and large single-event costs (60 for weather, 30 for animal herds) repeatedly empty the small pool, leaving the god incapacitated for ~70% of the year.
