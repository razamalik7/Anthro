# Task T5: Credit and Blame (Misinterpretation)

## What changed
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Credit.cs`:
  - Implemented `Region.InterpretStrikingEvent(x, y, isGood, source)`: On a striking divine or natural event, witnesses within 5000m who hold belief (`god:real > 0.05`) review deeds remembered within the last season (`Person.Deeds`, up to 90 days).
  - Deeds are evaluated for salience: recency, firsthand vividness, personal involvement, and dramatic weight of the act.
  - The most salient deed is attributed to the god: good event -> `god:<act>` reinforced (+1.0 target); bad/ominous event -> `god:<act>` penalized (-1.0 target). Learning rate is weighted by belief and credulity (`MindTrait.Credulity`).
  - Added `Simulation.GodActValue(Person p, Act act)` helper returning `Felt(GodAct(p, act)) * GodReal(p)` (or 0 when ablated).
- `Anthro.Sim/Activities/Decider.cs`: Believers incorporate `GodActValue` into evaluations for `Verb.Ask`, `Verb.Take`, `Verb.Attack`, and `Ambush`.
- `Anthro.Sim/JointActs.cs`: Incorporated `GodActValue` into group/gang taking (`Verb.Rally`) and raids.
- `Anthro.Sim/Society.cs`:
  - Added `GodActValue(p, Act.Confront)` into `ConfrontValue`.
  - Added `Remember(deed.Actor, deed, true)` so actors remember what they did and can tie omens to their own deeds.
  - Made `HappenHere(Deed)` public for cross-band and test usage.
- `Anthro.Sim/God/God.cs`: Hooked `InterpretStrikingEvent` into `OmenCommand` (negative portent), `BountyCommand` (positive blessing), and `WeatherCommand` (positive or negative based on delta).
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Prayers.cs`: Hooked `InterpretStrikingEvent` into prayer resolution (`PrayerAnswered` = good, `PrayerFailed` = bad).
- `Anthro.Tests/SimulationTests.cs`: Added unit test `OmenAfterWitnessedTakeDropsGodTakeAndSuppressesTaking` verifying that an ominous sign right after a witnessed take causes `god:take` to drop below 0 and applies a negative penalty to subsequent take options.

## Hand-set numbers disclosed
- **Salience weights** (`Credit.cs`):
  - Recency: $1.0 - (\text{ageDays} / 90) \times 0.75$.
  - Firsthand observation: $1.0\times$ firsthand vs $0.6\times$ hearsay.
  - Involvement: $1.5\times$ if actor, target, or own hearth; $1.0\times$ for neutral bystanders.
  - Dramatic acts: $1.4\times$ for Attack and Take; $1.0\times$ for Ask, Give, Confront.
  - Amount scaling: $1.0 + \min(2.0, |\text{Amount}|)$.
  - Minimum salience cutoff to form an association: $0.2$.
- **Belief absorption rate** (`Credit.cs`):
  - $\text{rate} = 0.15 \times (0.4 + \text{Credulity} \times 0.8) \times \text{GodReal}$.
- **Decider integration** (`GOD-LAYER.md` §5):
  - Added valuation: $\text{Felt}(\text{god:act}) \times \text{god:real} = (\text{god:act} \times 0.6) \times \text{god:real}$.

## Switches
- `Simulation.Off("credit")`: Disables credit and blame misinterpretation.
- `Simulation.Off("god")`: Disables all god-layer mechanics.

## Surprises & Diagnostic Notes
- `Person.Deeds` was already being communicated across camps via `Society.Gossip` and `SocialLearning.Exchange`, meaning once a god association (`god:<act>`) forms, it diffuses organically through camp conversation without requiring bespoke synchronization logic.
- Ensuring the actor remembers their own deeds in `Society.Happen` ensures that when a striking omen appears, the actor also reflects upon their own actions.
