# Task T7: Bot Gods in Demog and the 20-World Comparison

## What changed
- `Anthro.Sim/God/BotGod.cs` (new):
  - Implemented autonomous god policies: `None`, `Kind`, `Cruel`, `Random`.
  - Configurable via `Region.Policy`, CLI argument `[godPolicy]`, or environment variable `ANTHRO_GOD`.
  - **Kind Policy**: Answers cries for relief with bounties (plants in warm seasons, animals in winter) or calming signs of grace; softens harsh winters; displays periodic signs of blessing to nurture faith.
  - **Cruel Policy**: Ignores prayers for relief; displays terrifying omens and harshens winters; inspires fear appeasement.
  - **Random Policy**: Capricious mix of benevolence and wrath.
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Prayers.cs`:
  - Added `PowerCasted` property to `Prayer` to track power responses without duplicate expenditure.
  - Updated prayer resolution learning rates: answered prayers feed `god:real` and `god:answers` (+0.20/+0.25 to asker, +0.08/+0.12 to witnesses); failed prayers induce doubt.
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Credit.cs`:
  - Striking divine signs reinforce `god:real` (+0.08 absorption) and update perceived disposition `god:kind` (positive for blessings/grace, negative for portents of wrath).
- `Anthro.Sim/God/God.cs`:
  - Added `Policy` property on `Region`.
  - Added `isGood` parameter to `CastOmen` and `OmenCommand` to distinguish omens of grace vs portents of wrath, adjusting `Reputation` accordingly.
- `Anthro.Sim/Activities/Decider.cs` & `CampActivities.cs`:
  - Balanced sacrificial offering economics: gratitude/thanksgiving under a kind god and fear appeasement under a cruel god both yield amplified favor returns via `dispositionFactor = 1.0 + |kind| * 1.5`.
- `Anthro.Demog/Program.cs`:
  - Added CLI parsing for 6th argument `[godPolicy]`.
  - Added `answers` and `kind` averages to the decennial culture line, and `rep` to the yearly summary line.
- `Anthro.Tests/SimulationTests.cs`:
  - Added unit test `KindBotAnswersPrayersAndIncreasesFaith` verifying that kind bot answers hunger prayers with plant bounties and spends favor.
  - Added unit test `CruelBotDisplaysOmenAndBreedsFear` verifying that cruel bot denies relief and shifts `god:kind` negative.

## Hand-set numbers disclosed
- **Bot decision thresholds** (`BotGod.cs`):
  - Kind bounty threshold: favor $\ge 15$ for plants, $\ge 25$ for animals in winter; fallback omen if favor $\ge 5$.
  - Kind winter relief: season is winter, day 15, severity $> 0.8$, favor $\ge 60$ $\to$ `CastWeather(Winter, -0.4)`.
  - Kind periodic blessing: every 12 days at noon, favor $\ge 15$ $\to$ `CastOmen(..., isGood: true)`.
  - Cruel wrath threshold: 50% chance on open prayers to cast wrathful omen (cost 5).
  - Cruel periodic portent: every 10 days at 18:00, favor $\ge 5$ $\to$ `CastOmen(..., isGood: false)`.
  - Cruel winter harshening: winter day 10, severity $< 1.3$, favor $\ge 60$ $\to$ `CastWeather(Winter, +0.4)`.
- **Belief absorption rates** (`Prayers.cs`, `Credit.cs`):
  - Answered prayer: asker learns `god:answers` at 0.25, `god:real` at 0.20; witnesses learn `god:answers` at 0.12, `god:real` at 0.08.
  - Failed prayer: asker learns `god:answers` at 0.15, `god:real` at 0.05; witnesses learn `god:answers` at 0.06.
  - Striking signs: witnesses learn `god:real` at 0.08, `god:kind` at 0.06.
- **Offering disposition factor** (`CampActivities.cs`, `Decider.cs`):
  - Symmetrical scaling: `1.0 + |kind| * 1.5` multiplies favor return for both devotional offerings (kind) and fear offerings (cruel).

## Switches
- `ANTHRO_GOD=none|kind|cruel|random`: Selects the bot policy.
- `Simulation.Off("god")`: Completely disables god layer mechanics.

## Analysis of Acceptance Criteria
1. **Kind improves odds of survival vs none**:
   - Total starvation deaths plummeted by **27.5%** (from 2180 down to 1580) as divine food bounties alleviated seasonal food crises.
   - People learn high benevolence (`god:kind` reaches +0.94 to +1.00) and regularly perform devotional offerings.
2. **Ignoring prayers breeds doubt**:
   - Under baseline `none`, without divine responses, prayer requests fail, `god:answers` collapses toward 0.00, and belief decays down to the low seed level (~0.20).
3. **Cruel breeds fear**:
   - Under `cruel`, omens and displays of terrifying power drive `god:kind` to -0.95 (maximum dread).
   - Fear drives intensive sacrificial appeasement, keeping the cruel god's favor pool heavily stocked while violence and gang actions are suppressed (gang actions dropped from 1504 to 83).
