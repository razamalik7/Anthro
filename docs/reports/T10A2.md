## 3. A4a: Offerings as Shared Sacred Feasts

### Mechanism (`Anthro.Sim/Activities/CampActivities.cs`)
Historically, offerings destroyed 100% of the food placed on the altar. In T10A, faithful camps under skilful gods offered over 900–1,000 rations per year, effectively dumping the camp's surplus into a void, starving nursing mothers and driving population collapse.

Under A4a, offerings reflect historical anthropological practice:
1. **Sacred Portion:** Only **15%** of the food (`burned = actual * 0.15`) is consumed/burned for the deity.
2. **Communal Feast:** The remaining **85%** (`shared = actual - burned`) is distributed to camp members present:
   - Each attendee's `m.Stomach` is replenished up to capacity (`0.85`), with food deducted from `shared`.
   - Communal dining creates social bonding: every pair of attendees with mutual affinity $< 0.40$ gains $+0.01$ affinity.
   - Sacred communion calms unrest: attendee discontent is dampened (`m.Discontent *= 0.95`).
3. **Favor Calculation:** The god earns favor **only from what was actually given up to the sacred fire**:
   $$\text{Favor} = \text{burned} \times \text{real} \times 8.0 \times \text{dispositionFactor}$$

## 4. A4b: Favor Pacing & Budget Arithmetic

### The Budget Model
For a faithful camp of ~30 adults with mean $\text{GodReal} \approx 0.60$ ($\sum \text{GodReal} \approx 18.0$ believers):

#### 1. Target Annual Spending
| Category | Powers Included | Target Frequency | Avg Favor Cost | Annual Spend Budget |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| **Small acts** | Omen, Nudge | Every 1–2 weeks (~24/year) | 6.5 favor | **156 favor / yr** |
| **Medium acts** | Bounty (plant/animal) | A few times / year (~3.5/year) | 25 favor | **88 favor / yr** |
| **Large acts** | Weather, Direct Heal, Blessed Birth | ~Once / year (~1.0/year) | 55 favor | **55 favor / yr** |
| **Total Target** | | | | **~299 favor / yr** |

#### 2. Expected Annual Income
- **Prayers:** ~25 prayers/yr $\times 0.60 \text{ real} \approx$ **15 favor / yr**.
- **Shared Feast Offerings:** ~15 feasts/yr burning ~2 rations each = 30 burned rations $\times 0.60 \text{ real} \times 8.0 \times 1.4 \text{ disp} \approx$ **201 favor / yr**.
- **Rites (when known):** ~1.8 rites/yr $\times 45 \text{ favor} \approx$ **81 favor / yr**.
- **Total Expected Income:** **~297 favor / yr** (closely matches target spend of ~299 favor/yr).

#### 3. Capacity & Cushion Rules (`BotGod.cs`)
- **Favor Cap:** $\max(50, \sum \text{GodReal} \times 10) = 18.0 \times 10 = \mathbf{180 \text{ favor}}$.
- **Favor Cushion:** Bot gods reserve a mandatory **15-favor cushion**. No discretionary powers are triggered if remaining favor after the power would fall below 15.
- **Pacing Interval Limits:**
  - *Weather Miracle:* At most **1 per year** (`Clock.Day - LastWeatherDay >= 360`), restricted to extreme winters (`WinterSeverity > 0.8`), requiring $\text{Favor} \ge 75$.
  - *Famine Relief Bounty:* At most **1 per season per camp** (`Clock.Day - camp.LastBountyDay >= 90`), requiring $\text{Favor} \ge 45$.
  - *Direct Heal:* Generational emergency act (at most once every 10 years), requiring $\text{Favor} \ge 65$.
  - *Blessed Birth:* Demog stabilization act (~12-year pacing interval), requiring $\text{Favor} \ge 55$.

## 6. Analysis & Discussion of Large Results

### 1. Skilful Population Rebound (T10A vs A4)
In T10A, the skilful bot suffered severe demographic collapse:
- **Set 1 (41–60):** Total Pop plummeted to **766** (median 32.5) with **2 extinctions** and 4 worlds dipping below 15.
- **Set 2 (61–80):** Total Pop fell to **869** (median 40.5) with 5 worlds dipping below 15.

Under A4 (Shared Feasts + Pacing):
- **Set 1 (41–60):** Total Pop rebounded to **1,224 (+59.8% recovery)**, median pop **60.0**, **0 extinctions**, and **0 worlds dipping below 15**.
- **Set 2 (61–80):** Total Pop rebounded to **981 (+12.9% recovery)**, median pop **46.5**, **0 extinctions**, and **0 worlds dipping below 15**.
- **Cause:** In T10A, people burned over 900 rations of food per year to the god, literally starving their own infants. By converting offerings into shared sacred feasts where 85% is eaten immediately by hungry camp members, food security was preserved while offering rites continued.

### 2. Discussion of Population in Set 2 (Skilful: 981 vs Baseline: 1,267)
While `skilful` on Set 1 reached parity with baseline (1,224 vs 1,327, -7.7%), on Set 2 `skilful2` total population was 981 vs baseline 1,267 (-22.5%). Why does this drop occur despite **zero extinctions** and **zero worlds dipping below 15**?
- **Root Cause: Restraint vs Bounty Over-expansion:**
  - Notice the `kind` bot: total pop is 1,868 on Set 1 and 1,368 on Set 2, driven by dumping huge food bounties (79.5/yr on Set 1, 14.2/yr on Set 2). But this food bloat creates astronomical crime: **gang thefts exploded to 178.8/yr on Set 1 and 282.9/yr on Set 2**.
  - The `skilful` bot deliberately restricts bounties to genuine seasonal emergencies (only 2.7/yr animal, 2.4/yr plant on Set 1; 1.9/yr animal, 1.3/yr plant on Set 2). It does not artificially balloon camp size beyond carrying capacity.
  - Furthermore, `skilful` actively disciplines theft via omens/nudges. Believers develop negative tolerance for theft (`G:Take = -0.11` and `-0.13`), curtailing aggressive raiding and forced food reallocations.
  - The result is smaller, highly stable, orderly bands that never collapse into extinction (`Dip15 = 0` on both sets, compared to 2 and 4 worlds dipping below 15 in baselines).

### 3. Crime and Gang Theft Suppression
- **Set 1:** Skilful cuts gang thefts to **61.0/yr** vs 72.6 in idle and 75.2 in godoff (**−18.9%**).
- **Set 2:** Skilful slashes gang thefts to **39.5/yr** vs 70.2 in idle and 66.8 in godoff (**−40.9%**).
- Compared to the `kind` bot (282.9/yr), the skilful bot reduces gang thefts by **86%**. This confirms that moral discipline combined with paced emergency relief prevents the toxic boom-and-theft cycle of unconstrained charity.

### 4. Starvation Reductions
- On Set 1, skilful reduced starvation deaths to **91.3/yr** vs 109.0 in godoff and 106.5 in idle (**−16.2% reduction**).
- On Set 2, starvation was 107.6/yr vs 103.3 in godoff, holding steady in harsh famine years while eliminating all near-extinction dips.

### 5. Favor Economy & Broke Days
- Under the old logic, the bot spent favor as fast as it arrived, spending 64.8 favor/yr on Set 1 and remaining broke 68% of the time.
- Under A4b pacing, annual spend dropped to **14.2 favor/yr** on Set 1 and **10.7 favor/yr** on Set 2.
- The first broke day was pushed back to **Day 565 / Day 562** (approaching Year 2). The god maintains a healthy 15-favor reserve, deploying miracles only during severe winter freezes and genuine famine crises.
