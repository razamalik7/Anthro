# Task T4: Powers (Omen, Bounty, Weather, Blessed Birth)

## What changed
- `Anthro.Sim/God/God.cs`: Implemented four natural divine power commands:
  - `OmenCommand` (Cost 5.0): Emits an omen near $(x, y)$, witnessed by grown adults within 5000m. Witnesses absorb `god:real` (+0.08 toward 1.0). Writes to Chronicle and records `GodEvent`.
  - `BountyCommand` (Cost 20.0 plants, 30.0 animals):
    - Plants: Restocks plant cells within 3000m up to $1.5\times$ capacity, refills river fish stock to $1.2\times$ capacity, restocks wood, raises god reputation by +1.0.
    - Animals: Increases count of existing huntable herds within 5000m, sets condition to 1.0 (fat/well-fed), removes fear/shyness, steers them toward $(x, y)$, or spawns a new herd if none are nearby. Raises god reputation by +1.0.
  - `WeatherCommand` (Cost 60.0): Directly shapes seasonal climate. Softening (+delta) adds to `Climate.SeasonOverride`, mitigates `Climate.WinterSeverity`, boosts nut/berry/grain/fish crops by $(1 + 0.3 \cdot \delta)$, and adds +1.5 reputation. Harshening (-delta) chills the season and subtracts -1.5 reputation.
  - `BlessedBirthCommand` (Cost 40.0): Marks an expecting mother's upcoming child with a divine blessing (`mother.BlessedNextBirth = true`).
- `Anthro.Sim/Ecology/EcologySystem.cs`: Added `SpawnGroup(Species, x, y, count, sim)` helper to spawn herds deterministically during divine bounties.
- `Anthro.Sim/World/Climate.cs`: Changed setters on `WinterSeverity`, `YearWarmth`, `Mast`, `Berries`, `Grain`, and `FishRun` to `internal set` for climate control by the god layer.
- `Anthro.Sim/People/Person.cs`: Added `BlessedNextBirth` and `Blessed` flags.
- `Anthro.Sim/Simulation.cs`: In `GiveBirth`, applied divine blessing: child is born with 1.0 health, boosted body traits (+0.3 Strength, Endurance, Immunity), boosted mind traits (+0.3 Boldness, Resilience, Aptitude), guaranteed zero complications, and major chronicle note.
- `Anthro.Sim/Region.cs`: Automatically resets `Climate.SeasonOverride = 0` at the start of each season in `Daily()`.
- `Anthro.Tests/SimulationTests.cs`: Added 4 unit tests covering every power:
  - `PowerOmenReinforcesBeliefInWitnesses`
  - `PowerBountyFlourishesPlantsAndAnimals`
  - `PowerWeatherModifiesClimateAndWinterSeverity`
  - `PowerBlessedBirthBestowsGiftedChild`

## Hand-set numbers disclosed
- **Power Costs** (`GOD-LAYER.md` §4):
  - Omen: 5.0 favor.
  - Bounty: 20.0 favor (plants), 30.0 favor (animals).
  - Weather: 60.0 favor / season.
  - Blessed Birth: 40.0 favor.
- **Ranges & Multipliers**:
  - Omen: 5000m witness radius, +0.08 belief absorption.
  - Plant Bounty: 3000m radius, $1.5\times$ plant capacity, $1.2\times$ fish capacity.
  - Animal Bounty: 5000m radius, herd replenishment $+ \max(3, \text{GroupMin})$, condition $1.0$.
  - Weather: $\text{SeasonOverride} += \delta \cdot 3.0$, Winter severity change $-\delta \cdot 0.8$, crop factor $(1 + 0.3 \cdot \delta)$.
  - Blessed Birth: Health $1.0$, complication risk $0.0$, $+0.3$ to Strength, Endurance, Immunity, Boldness, Resilience, Aptitude.

## Switches
- `Simulation.Off("power")`: Disables all god powers.
- `Simulation.Off("omen")`: Ablates omen power.
- `Simulation.Off("bounty")`: Ablates bounty power.
- `Simulation.Off("weather")`: Ablates weather power.
- `Simulation.Off("bless")`: Ablates blessed birth power.
- `Simulation.Off("god")`: Disables all god features.
