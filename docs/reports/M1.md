# Report: Task M1 — Paired Comparison, Data Hygiene & Favor Pacing Audit

## 1. Overview & Method Change
- **New Method Standard:** All comparisons from M1 onward use the **40-seed paired comparison tool** (`Anthro.Demog/tools/paired_cmp.py`).
- **Paired Log Ratio Arithmetic:**
  - For each seed $s \in [41, 80]$, pair condition $X_s$ against canonical `godoff` reference $Y_s$ (seeds 41–60 vs `godoff`, 61–80 vs `godoff2`).
  - Log ratio: $r_s = \ln(X_s / Y_s)$ for positive metrics; $r_s = \ln((X_s + 1) / (Y_s + 1))$ for count metrics with potential zeros (gang thefts, violent deaths).
  - **95% Bootstrap Confidence Interval:** 10,000 resamples with replacement over the 40 paired log ratios, mapped to percentage change: $(\exp(\bar{r}^*) - 1) \times 100\%$.
  - **Standard of Evidence:** Every claim is stated as `"+a% (95% CI b% to c%, 40 worlds)"`. An effect is **REAL** only if the 95% CI strictly excludes zero. If the CI spans zero, the difference is **NOISE**.

### What the Paired Analysis Overturns vs Confirms

1. **Skilful Demographic "Boom" is Noise:**
   - In T10B, Set 2 skilful had 1,539 pop (+21.5% over godoff), leading to claims of a major population surge.
   - The 40-seed paired test shows: **+1.7% (95% CI -18.6% to +26.9%, 40 worlds)**. The interval spans zero widely. Population growth is completely within baseline noise.
2. **Skilful Crime Suppression is Real and Substantial:**
   - Gang thefts: **-64.2% (95% CI -80.0% to -37.2%, 40 worlds)**. Zero is excluded; skilful omens and paced relief cut gang theft by nearly two-thirds.
   - Violent deaths: **-24.9% (95% CI -42.5% to -3.0%, 40 worlds)**. Zero is excluded; mortal violence drops by a quarter.
3. **Kind Bot Population Boom is Noise; Starvation Reduction is Real:**
   - Kind bot population: **+18.1% (95% CI -4.8% to +45.0%, 40 worlds)**. Spans zero; not a statistically reliable effect.
   - Starvation reduction: **-27.0% (95% CI -41.2% to -11.2%, 40 worlds)**. Statistically significant reduction in starvation.
4. **Idle Baseline Shift:**
   - Starvation in idle: **-6.5% (95% CI -14.5% to +2.3%, 40 worlds)** is indistinguishable from godoff.
   - However, idle population is lower: **-18.4% (95% CI -33.0% to -1.1%, 40 worlds)**, caused by members expending hours on unreciprocated prayers and unmanaged theft spirals in several seeds.

### Diagnosis: Which Side is Short?

The pacing target of ~300 favor/yr is missed by 94%. All three mechanisms contribute, but with very different responsibilities:

1. **Income is 2.1x to 3.5x short of the spending target:**
   - Total income earned across the 40 worlds averages **144.9 favor/yr** (Set 1: 48.0 favor/yr, Set 2: 241.9 favor/yr).
   - Steady daily income (prayers) produces only **6.8 favor/yr** (~0.019 favor/day).
   - Communal rites produce **0.0 favor/yr**.
   - Offerings produce **135.6 favor/yr**, but arrive in sudden, episodic crisis bursts rather than steady flows.
   - Even if the god spent 100% of income, income cannot sustain a 300 favor/yr budget.

2. **The Favor Cap truncates burst income and prevents banking:**
   - Favor cap formula: $\text{Cap} = \max(50.0, \sum_{\text{believers}} \text{god:real} \times 10.0)$.
   - With typical fledgling cults ($\sum \text{real} \approx 5–8$), the cap sits at **50–80 favor**.
   - When a famine hits and desperate families offer food, an offering wave can generate 60–100 favor in hours. Because of `Favor = Math.Min(FavorCap, Favor + gain)`, excess favor over the cap is immediately lost.
   - Furthermore, the cap prevents the god from slowly banking favor during 3 years of calm to afford a 60-favor weather miracle.

3. **The Bot Logic holds back aggressively (the primary bottleneck on spending):**
   - Even out of the **144.9 favor/yr** that arrives, the skilful bot spends only **17.5 favor/yr (only 12.1% of its income!)**.
   - **Why does the bot refuse to spend?**
     - **15-Favor Cushion:** The bot reserves 15 favor at all times, raising the effective barrier to act: a 5-favor omen requires 20 favor; a 30-favor bounty requires 45 favor; a 60-favor weather miracle requires 75 favor.
     - **Narrow Activation Windows:**
       - *Weather:* Restricted strictly to late autumn (`DayOfSeason >= 75`), requires `WinterSeverity > 0.8` (only ~15–20% of winters), and requires `Favor >= 75` (above the favor cap in many worlds). Across all 40 worlds, Weather was cast only 5 times total (0.0025/yr/world).
       - *Heal:* Requires illness health < 0.35, Rep $\ge 4.0$, believers $\ge 5$, a 10-year cooldown (`Clock.Day - LastHealDay >= 3600`), and `Favor >= 65`. Cast in only 6 of 40 worlds.
       - *BlessedBirth:* Modulo condition `Day % 4320 == 0` (12-year tick) and `Favor >= 55`. Cast in only 4 of 40 worlds.
       - *Bounties:* Requires active prayer + acute starvation + 90-day cooldown + `Favor >= 35–45`.
   - **Conclusion on Bottleneck:** While income is ~2x short of 300, the bot is **8x short of spending its own income**. The restrictive cushions and coincident trigger conditions prevent discretionary spending from firing during normal band life.
