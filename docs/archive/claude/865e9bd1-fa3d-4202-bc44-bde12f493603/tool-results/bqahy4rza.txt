# Task T3: Praying and Requests

## What changed
- `Anthro.Sim/Activities/Activity.cs`: Added `Verb.Pray`.
- `Anthro.Sim/Activities/CampActivities.cs`: Added `Pray : TimedActivity` (1 hour duration). When chosen, invokes `Simulation.RegisterPrayer(p, need, target)`.
- `Anthro.Sim/Activities/Decider.cs`: Added option to pray during severe crises when `god:real > 0` and `god:answers > 0`. Constrained to severe desperation (extreme hunger, illness, severe injury, grief) and only if no unresolved prayer is already open for this person.
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Prayers.cs`: Created `Prayer` model with `PrayerNeed` (`Hunger`, `Sickness`, `Danger`, `Grief`), resolution tracking via `Region.ResolvePrayers()`, give-up timeouts when pleas go unmet, belief adjustments for asker and witnesses upon resolution, and favor generation on prayer.
- `Anthro.Sim/Region.cs`: Integrated `ResolvePrayers()` into `Daily()` and yearly stats reset.
- `Anthro.Sim/Simulation.cs`: Added prayer metrics to `YearStats` (`PrayersAsked`, `PrayersResolvedGood`, `PrayersResolvedBad`, `FavorGain`).
- `Anthro.Demog/Program.cs`: Displayed yearly prayer totals `prayers asked/answered/failed favor`.
- `Anthro.Tests/SimulationTests.cs`: Added unit tests for prayer coincidence raising faith and prayer failure spreading doubt.

## Hand-set numbers disclosed
- **Crisis thresholds to consider prayer** (`Decider.cs`):
  - Hunger: `Satiety < 0.25` AND `Home.FoodTotal < 0.5` AND `Band.FoodTotal < 1.0` (severe famine, pantry bare).
  - Sickness: `Illness != null` AND `Sickness > 0.3` (serious debilitating sickness).
  - Danger/Injury: `Injury > 0.4` (badly wounded).
  - Grief: `Grief > 0.6` (mourning recent loss).
- **Prayer utility score** (`Decider.cs`):
  - `score = need * real * answers`. A person with 0 belief never prays. A person only prays when desperate need weighted by belief exceeds other mundane options.
- **Prayer resolution & give-up windows** (`Prayers.cs`):
  - Hunger: resolved good if `Satiety >= 0.75` within 5 days; fails if 5 days elapse without food or asker dies.
  - Sickness: resolved good if `Sickness <= 0` and `Health >= 0.75`; fails if 7 days elapse without recovery or target dies.
  - Danger: resolved good if `Injury <= 0.05` after 2 days; fails if 5 days elapse or asker dies.
  - Grief: resolved good if 15 days elapse and asker lives; fails if asker dies.
- **Belief absorption on resolution** (`Prayers.cs`):
  - Answered: Asker learns `god:answers` at weight 0.08, `god:real` at 0.05. Witnesses learn `god:answers` at 0.03, `god:real` at 0.01.
  - Failed: Asker learns `god:answers` at weight 0.08, `god:real` at 0.03 towards 0.0. Witnesses learn `god:answers` at 0.03 towards 0.0.
- **Favor grant on prayer** (`Prayers.cs`):
  - `Region.Favor += 1.0 * GodReal(p)` (capped at `FavorCap`).

## Switches
- `Simulation.Off("pray")`: Disables prayer generation and resolution.
- `Simulation.Off("god")`: Disables all god-layer mechanics.

## Surprises & Diagnostic Notes
- An initial unconstrained draft allowed people to pray whenever mildly hungry (`Satiety < 0.4`), resulting in daily meals being falsely credited as answered prayers. This caused runaway belief ($1.0$) and excessive praying ($3.9$ h/day), collapsing survival. Restricting prayer strictly to crises where normal options have failed and enforcing a give-up timeout when pleas are ignored completely restored natural behavior: without divine answers, unanswered prayers lower faith and belief decays naturally to 0.

# Task T4: Powers (Omen, Bounty, Weather, Blessed Birth)

## What changed
- `Anthro.Sim/God/God.cs`: Implemented four natural divine power commands:
  - `OmenCommand` (Cost 5.0): Emits an omen near $(x, y)$, witnessed by grown adults within 5000m. Witnesses absorb `god:real` (+0.08 toward 1.0). Writes to Chronicle and records `GodEvent`.
  - `BountyCommand` (Cost 20.0 plants, 30.0 animals):
    - Plants: Restocks plant cells within 3000m up to $1.5\times$ capacity, refills river fish stock to $1.2\times$ capacity, restocks wood, raises god reputation by +1.0.
    - Animals: Increases count of existing huntable herds within 5000m, sets condition to 1.0 (fat/well-fed), removes fear/shyness, steers them toward $(x, y)$, or spawns a new herd if none are nearby. Raises god reputation by +1.0.
  - `WeatherCommand` (Cost 60.0): Directly shapes seasonal climate. Softening (+delta) adds to `Climate.SeasonOverride`, mitigates `Climate.WinterSeverity`, boosts nut/berry/grain/fish crops by $(1 + 0.3 \cdot \delta)$, and adds +1.5 reputation. Harshening (-delta) chills the season and subtracts -1.5 reputation.
  - `BlessedBirthCommand` (Cost 40.0): Marks an expecting mother's upcoming child with a divine blessing (`mother.BlessedNextBirth = true`).
- `Anthro.Sim/Ecology/EcologySystem.cs`: Added `SpawnGroup(Species, x, y, count, sim)` helper to spawn herds deterministically during divine bounties.
- `Anthro.Sim/World/Climate.cs`: Changed setters on `WinterSeverity`, `YearWarmth`, `Mast`, `Berries`, `Grain`, and `FishRun` to `internal set` for climate control by the god layer.

# Task T5: Credit and Blame (Misinterpretation)

## What changed
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Credit.cs`:
  - Implemented `Region.InterpretStrikingEvent(x, y, isGood, source)`: On a striking divine or natural event, witnesses within 5000m who hold belief (`god:real > 0.05`) review deeds remembered within the last season (`Person.Deeds`, up to 90 days).
  - Deeds are evaluated for salience: recency, firsthand vividness, personal involvement, and dramatic weight of the act.
  - The most salient deed is attributed to the god: good event -> `god:<act>` reinforced (+1.0 target); bad/ominous event -> `god:<act>` penalized (-1.0 target). Learning rate is weighted by belief and credulity (`MindTrait.Credulity`).
  - Added `Simulation.GodActValue(Person p, Act act)` helper returning `Felt(GodAct(p, act)) * GodReal(p)` (or 0 when ablated).
- `Anthro.Sim/Activities/Decider.cs`: Believers incorporate `GodActValue` into evaluations for `Verb.Ask`, `Verb.Take`, `Verb.Attack`, and `Ambush`.
- `Anthro.Sim/JointActs.cs`: Incorporated `GodActValue` into group/gang taking (`Verb.Rally`) and raids.
- `Anthro.Sim/Society.cs`:
  - Added `GodActValue(p, Act.Confront)` into `ConfrontValue`.
  - Added `Remember(deed.Actor, deed, true)` so actors remember what they did and can tie omens to their own deeds.
  - Made `HappenHere(Deed)` public for cross-band and test usage.
- `Anthro.Sim/God/God.cs`: Hooked `InterpretStrikingEvent` into `OmenCommand` (negative portent), `BountyCommand` (positive blessing), and `WeatherCommand` (positive or negative based on delta).
- `Anthro.Sim/God/Prayers.cs`: Hooked `InterpretStrikingEvent` into prayer resolution (`PrayerAnswered` = good, `PrayerFailed` = bad).
- `Anthro.Tests/SimulationTests.cs`: Added unit test `OmenAfterWitnessedTakeDropsGodTakeAndSuppressesTaking` verifying that an ominous sign right after a witnessed take causes `god:take` to drop below 0 and applies a negative penalty to subsequent take options.

## Hand-set numbers disclosed
- **Salience weights** (`Credit.cs`):
  - Recency: $1.0 - (\text{ageDays} / 90) \times 0.75$.
  - Firsthand observation: $1.0\times$ firsthand vs $0.6\times$ hearsay.
  - Involvement: $1.5\times$ if actor, target, or own hearth; $1.0\times$ for neutral bystanders.
  - Dramatic acts: $1.4\times$ for Attack and Take; $1.0\times$ for Ask, Give, Confront.
  - Amount scaling: $1.0 + \min(2.0, |\text{Amount}|)$.
  - Minimum salience cutoff to form an association: $0.2$.
- **Belief absorption rate** (`Credit.cs`):
  - $\text{rate} = 0.15 \times (0.4 + \text{Credulity} \times 0.8) \times \text{GodReal}$.
- **Decider integration** (`GOD-LAYER.md` §5):
  - Added valuation: $\text{Felt}(\text{god:act}) \times \text{god:real} = (\text{god:act} \times 0.6) \times \text{god:real}$.
