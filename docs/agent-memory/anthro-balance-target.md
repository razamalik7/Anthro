Without a god, humans can persist but survival shouldn't be easy: a band of ~30 dying out is realistic,
and it gives the game stakes (user 2026-10-06: "survival maybe shouldnt be ez without the player").
They advance slowly without help: user 2026-10-06 judged 7-20 discovery stages in 40 years "much too
fast with no help". The god speeds up innovation and evolution, creates story/drama, rescues people,
improves their odds.

High violence is acceptable (good for the game) as long as its causes are logical, not buggy.

Game vision: partly a city builder — progression from semi-scattered camps toward more centralised
settlements as tech improves; player needn't watch everyone (1-2 person offshoots fine).

**How to apply:** don't tune toward "everyone survives"; check discovery pacing is slow without a god;
when judging violence, verify causes not just rates. Related: [[anthro-design-principles]],
[[anthro-realism-not-exactness]].
